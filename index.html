Do you offer fully bilingual service? +

Yes — every conversation, document, and explanation is available in English or Español. Astrid's first language is Spanish, and bilingual service is at the heart of Sonora.

Do you handle payroll for small businesses? +

Yes — payroll is a core service. We set up, process, and file payroll taxes for small businesses and growing teams. All bilingual.

Is the initial consultation free? +

Yes. Your first consultation is free and confidential — designed to understand your needs and outline a clear plan forward.
