About Us

A locally-owned cleaning company that’s been growing every year since 2006.

Carolina Commercial Cleaning Services Inc was established in 2006 as a residential cleaning company. As customer needs grew, the company expanded into post-construction cleaning, janitorial services, and commercial cleaning.

Since then, the company has continued to grow each year by adding services, employees, and satisfied customers.

Through the years, many cleaning companies have come and gone in the area. Carolina Commercial Cleaning has remained because of its commitment to customer satisfaction, quality work, and reasonable pricing.

The company believes in doing more than customers expect — and its greatest advertising comes from happy clients.

  • Family-rooted team Long-tenured employees who treat your space like their own.
  • Customer-first We do more than expected — happy clients are our best advertising.
  • Quality that lasts Detailed scopes, supervised crews, and consistent results since 2006.
